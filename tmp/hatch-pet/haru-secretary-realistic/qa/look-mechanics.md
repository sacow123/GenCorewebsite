# Haru look mechanics

Haru keeps her hips, feet, clipboard, and lower torso anchored. Her eyes lead each look, followed by a subtle head-and-neck turn and a restrained shoulder shift; her hair follows softly. The clipboard stays held close and becomes slightly more side-on with a head turn.

- `000` up: pupils, eyelids, chin, and head angle lift gently; shoulders stay centered.
- `090` screen-right: nose tip and pupils move toward screen-right; the screen-left side of her face is more visible, and the clipboard is slightly side-on.
- `180` down: pupils, eyelids, chin, and upper torso tip down naturally toward the clipboard.
- `270` screen-left: nose tip and pupils move toward screen-left; the screen-right side of her face is more visible, and the clipboard shifts subtly with the torso.

The intermediate steps must interpolate these cardinal poses evenly at 22.5-degree intervals. Do not rotate, skew, or tilt the whole sprite.
