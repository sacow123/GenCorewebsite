You can select this template,

  • The part type is the Abutment crown bridge that is applied the Megalink system Interface
  • The materials is Zirconia Disk/Block
  • Setting the Abutment base lines is mandatory.
  • The Emergence lines are optional, but it is recommended for better texture quality around interfaces.
