Replace B-axis Timing Belt

M AI° Drive Unit Repair Guide

1. Symptom

During homing, the B-axis coordinates continue to change but the physical axis does not move.

2. Preparation

Clear the milling table

Remove everything from the workpiece platform in the machining chamber and clean it thoroughly.

Required tool: M2.5 hex driver

3. Procedure

1
At the Ready status
2
Position the milling table

In Axis jog mode, position the spindle inward and rotate it more than 180° in the A- direction to make the cover easy to remove.

⚠️ CautionBecause homing cannot be completed, the exact position coordinates may not be displayed. Check visually and work carefully to avoid collisions.
3
Remove the cover
4
Remove the damaged B-axis timing belt
5
Check the pulley for sharp or pointed structures
6
Mark the tensioner position
7
Slightly loosen the tensioner fastening screw so that the tensioner can move.
8
Install the B-axis timing belt

Turn the pulley by hand and check that the belt engages and rotates without slipping.

9
Adjust timing-belt tension

Move the tensioner to the marked position, adjust the timing-belt tension, and tighten the bolt at the proper position. Check the tension level.

10
Check B-axis operation

On the Axis jog page, move the B-axis several times in the + and - directions and check for noise or malfunction.

11
Apply Loctite to the screw holes
12
Install the cover in the correct direction

Proceed in the order of temporary fastening → position adjustment → final tightening, and make sure there is no step at the top.

13
Move the A-axis to a roughly level position
14
Run homing and verify that it completes. The procedure is complete.
